06 · Before you plant

What can go wrong

Price moves

A position is half SOL and half coin. If the coin falls, you end up holding more of it, so you can get back less SOL than you put in even after fees. Every farm shows what your position would be worth after a price move before you plant.

Coins can die

Most memecoins go to zero eventually. Fees earned on a coin that collapses rarely make up for the drop. Only plant what you're fine losing.

The fee tier

pump.fun pools pay LPs 0.20% of each trade once the coin is above about 420 SOL market cap, and 0.02% below it. AFK reads each pool's live rate from PumpSwap.

Yield is a pace

Yield / day is the last 24 hours, not a forecast. Volume changes every minute, and your share shrinks when others add after you.

Where does the money come from?

From trading fees. Every PumpSwap trade pays a fee and part of it, the LP fee, stays in the pool for the people providing its liquidity. Your share of the pool is your share of those fees. AFK reads each pool's current rate from PumpSwap's on-chain fee config.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Your position holds half SOL and half coin. If the coin falls, the pool sells you more of it on the way down, so you can end up with less SOL than you put in, even after fees. This is called impermanent loss and it is the main risk.

Does AFK hold my funds?

No. AFK builds a standard PumpSwap transaction, checks it in a simulation, and your own wallet signs it. LP tokens go straight to your wallet. AFK has no contract of its own and cannot move your funds.

What does AFK charge?

Planting and harvesting are free on AFK. You only pay Solana network fees and a small, refundable rent for new token accounts.

Do I need to claim anything?

No. Fees stay in the pool and your LP tokens are worth more of it as trades come in. When you harvest, you get your share including the fees.

Which pools can I farm?

Canonical PumpSwap pools of pump.fun coins paired with SOL. AFK only lists pools it finds and checks on-chain, so copycat pools never make the board. Paste any coin address to find its pool. Coins still on the bonding curve have no pool yet.

Why did my plant fail?

Usually the price moved more than your limit between the quote and the transaction landing. Nothing is taken except the network fee. Try again, or allow a bigger price move on fast coins.

Can I use coins I already hold?

Yes. Open the farm and use "Pair coins": AFK adds the coins you hold together with the matching SOL, without buying more.
