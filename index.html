FAQ

Quick answers

Where does the money come from?

Trading fees. Part of every PumpSwap trade stays in the pool for its liquidity providers. Your share of the pool is your share of those fees.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Your farm is half SOL, half coin. If the coin drops you can get back less SOL than you put in, even after fees. Each farm shows this before you plant.

Does AFK hold my funds?

No. Your wallet signs a standard PumpSwap transaction and the LP tokens go to your wallet.

What does AFK charge?

Nothing. Only Solana network fees and refundable account rent.

Is yield / day a promise?

No. It's the last 24 hours of real fees divided by the pool size. Volume changes every minute.

Which pools work?

Official PumpSwap pools of pump.fun coins paired with SOL. Paste any coin address to find its pool.
